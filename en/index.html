Jason Holmes

Managing Partner

Combines entrepreneurial understanding with a clear eye for people and structures. His focus is on filling demanding roles in the construction and real estate industry.

Jason Holmes is Managing Partner and founder of Holmes Consulting Partners GmbH, based in Berlin — a recruitment consultancy specialising in the construction and real estate industry, which he founded together with his wife Paulin Holmes.

After several years as a property valuer it became clear to him early on that what fascinated him most was not the buildings but the people behind them — those who develop projects, steer portfolios and move markets. That realisation, together with a little luck and coincidence, led him into specialist recruitment in 2016.

At two of the leading recruitment firms in the construction and real estate sector he gained extensive market experience and took on growing responsibility. For roughly the last five of those years he played a key part in building and developing the Berlin office of his then employer — an experience that gave him a deep understanding of growing organisations, market structures and client requirements, and laid the groundwork for the next logical step.

Founding his own company together with Paulin Holmes was exactly that step. More than 300 successfully filled positions across Germany — from specialist professionals through to leadership roles in asset, centre, construction, fund, investment, leasing, portfolio, project, property and transaction management, project leadership, project control, research & analysis, and the development of property across all asset classes.

His approach combines structured, highly efficient, data-informed processes with a strong grasp of the market, human intuition and a nationwide network built personally over many years across every relevant segment of the industry. What sets Jason Holmes apart is the combination of a real estate background of his own, excellent market knowledge and an analytical, strategic approach — paired with the understanding that behind every appointment stands a person with an individual skill set, a unique personality and particular wishes. Data-informed market analysis, a precise feel for structures and requirements and a deep understanding of long-term fit between candidates and companies form the foundation of his work. This interplay of expertise, method and humanity creates a quality of advice that convinces companies of every size as well as candidates, and lays the basis for working together with openness, reliability and genuine shared success.

Jason Holmes studied real estate management at HTW Berlin, including a semester abroad at QUT in Brisbane, Australia. He lives in Berlin with his wife and two children and speaks German and English.

Paulin Holmes

Managing Partner

Brings years of experience in direct approach and in building lasting networks. Specialised in precise appointments and long-term partnerships.

Paulin Holmes is co-founder and Managing Partner of Holmes Consulting Partners GmbH, a recruitment consultancy specialising in the construction and real estate industry.

Through her degree in real estate management and her professional experience in property and asset management, she knows the commercial structures and demands of the industry first hand. Those insights still shape her understanding of which skills and profiles the sector is looking for.

For several years she also worked within a recruitment firm specialising in real estate, where she was involved above all in back office, research, approaching new clients and looking after existing contacts.

At Holmes Consulting Partners, Paulin Holmes is responsible above all for the organisational and operational side of the business, for structuring internal processes and for relationships with new and existing business partners.

Her open, communicative manner and her enjoyment of networking keep personal exchange within the industry alive and build long-term relationships with clients and partners.

Together with Jason Holmes she pursues the goal of bringing companies in the construction and real estate industry together with the right people for the long term, and of building partnerships based on trust.

Frequently asked questions

Which areas of construction and real estate do you specialise in?
We fill specialist and leadership roles across the entire value chain — from project development and site management through to asset, property and fund management.
How long does an appointment usually take?
As a rule we allow 8 to 12 weeks, from the first briefing to a successful appointment. Pace always follows quality.
Do you also handle confidential searches?
Yes — discretion is the basis of our work, towards companies and candidates alike.
How does the recruitment process work?
In clear, structured steps: briefing and role profile, direct approach, interviews and shortlisting, personality analysis, and presentation through to decision and appointment.
What does it cost companies?
We discuss the specific terms individually in the first conversation.
What makes you different from job boards?
We do not place superficially, we advise. Instead of scattering applications we rely on direct approach, deep sector understanding and the personal fit between people and companies.
What happens if an appointment does not work out?
If it does not work out within the agreed guarantee period, we handle the replacement free of charge. For us an appointment only counts as successful once it works long term.
Do you work across Germany only, or internationally as well?
Our core network covers Germany. Where needed we also search internationally, particularly for leadership roles and international investors.
Are there any costs for candidates?
No. Our advice is always free of charge for candidates. That includes using our personality analysis tool.
Do I have to be actively job hunting to talk to you?
No. Many of the most interesting roles come out of confidential conversations with people who are not actively looking at all. A no-obligation conversation is always worthwhile.
How can I use the Job Fit personality analysis?
You can take the analysis at any time, free and without obligation. Whether you then keep the results to yourself or bring them into a recruitment process with us is up to you.
