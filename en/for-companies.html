This is how we work with you.

An appointment is only as good as the process behind it. At Holmes Consulting Partners every search follows a structured, data-informed path — with people always at the centre.

First conversation & needs analysis

It starts with an open conversation. We get to know your company — your structures, your culture, your goals. And we ask the right questions: which skills do you genuinely need? Which personality will fit your team for the long run? Only once we understand that does the real work begin.

1
2

A precise role profile

Building on that conversation, we develop a detailed search profile together — technically sound and culturally considered. Our sector experience goes straight into it: we think along, challenge assumptions and sharpen the profile so the search is focused from day one. We also recommend our Talent Matcher tool to make the profile as precise as possible.

Structured research

With the approved profile we begin a systematic market search. We combine the network we have built over years with modern sourcing methods and an extensive database, giving you a complete and reliable view of the market. On an exclusive assignment we review the longlist openly with you before making any approach.

3
4

Personal & discreet direct approach

We approach potential candidates individually, confidentially and always as equals. We communicate clearly, create genuine interest and make sure we also reach those who are not actively looking — but open to the right move.

In-depth assessment

We hold detailed personal interviews with every promising candidate. This goes beyond qualifications: we look at motivation, personality and long-term fit with your company. The result is a well-founded, honest assessment — not a polished CV.

5
6

A curated shortlist

You receive a clear selection of the strongest profiles, well structured and with our personal assessment and concrete recommendation. We discuss the candidates openly with you and decide the next steps together.

Interview support

We handle all interview scheduling and prepare both sides properly. Throughout the process we act as your sounding board — with assessments, feedback and a clear position right up to the final decision.

7
8

Offer & transition

Once the decision is made we actively support the contract stage — from advising on terms to taking references and helping with the candidate’s current employment. Our aim: a secure, smooth start from day one.

A lasting partnership

Our work does not end when the contract is signed. We stay in touch, support onboarding and remain available as a long-term partner. Good recruitment is measured not by a quick close, but by lasting success.
