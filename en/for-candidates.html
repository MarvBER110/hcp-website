How we support you
step by step

Straightforward, discreet, transparent and always built around your goals.

A career move is one of the most important decisions of your working life. We take that seriously and guide you through it the way we would want to be guided ourselves: openly, discreetly, professionally and with genuine interest in your situation.

A first conversation — no pressure, no obligation

The first step is simple. You get in touch — with a concrete idea, a quiet wish for change, or simply out of curiosity. We listen. No standard questionnaire, no sales pitch. Just an honest exchange about where you are and where you want to go.

1
2

We get to know you properly

A CV says a lot, but far from everything. So we take the time to understand you as a person: your strengths, your wishes, your values, what a good working environment looks like to you. Only then can we identify roles that fit not just on paper, but day to day.

Access to roles you will not find

Many positions are never advertised. Through our nationwide network in the construction and real estate industry we have access to vacancies and companies that would otherwise stay invisible to you. We get you into the conversation — deliberately, not at random.

3
4

An honest briefing before every interview

Before you meet a company, you know what to expect. We prepare you thoroughly: who will you be talking to? What drives the company? Where are the potential pitfalls? What is the salary range? You walk into every interview informed and confident.

Support throughout the process

We do not disappear after the first interview. We accompany you through every round, give you honest feedback and act as your sounding board — including in the difficult moments.

5
6

Support right up to your first day

After you sign we stay by your side: with contract details, onboarding and every question around the move — up to a successful start and beyond.

A relationship that lasts

Our interest in you does not end when the contract is signed. We stay in touch, follow your development and remain available to you long term. Good advice does not stop at the placement.
